## Neighborhood Slang & Nicknames

| Nickname | Actual Name | Location | Notes |
|----------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **East Nasty** | East Nashville | East of Cumberland River | Affectionate term for the edgy, artsy area. Vogue called it "Nashville's coolest neighborhood" in 2016. |
| **WeHo** | Wedgewood-Houston | South of Downtown | Arts district with galleries, breweries, distilleries. Former warehouse district. |
| **SoBro** | South of Broadway | South of Broadway, east of Gulch | Music City Center, Country Music Hall of Fame, Johnny Cash Museum |
| **The Gulch** | The Gulch | Southwest of Downtown | LEED-certified trendy area, high-rise condos, Station Inn |
| **12 South** | 12th Avenue South | South Nashville near Belmont | "I Believe in Nashville" mural, boutiques, walkable |
| **The Nations** | The Nations | West Nashville off 51st Ave | Former industrial, now hip with breweries and cafes |
| **NashVegas** | Nashville | Whole city | Locals often cringe at this - tourists use it, refers to party atmosphere. Locals prefer "Music City" |
| **Smashville** | Bridgestone Arena / Preds fan culture | Downtown | Nashville Predators hockey fandom |
| **Music City** | Nashville | Whole city | Preferred local name, references music history |
| **Athens of the South** | Nashville | Whole city | References the Parthenon replica in Centennial Park |
| **Batman Building** | AT&T Tower | Downtown | 33-story skyscraper, tallest in TN, profile resembles Batman's cowl |
| **Mother Church** | Ryman Auditorium | Downtown | Historic venue, former home of Grand Ole Opry |
| **Honky-Tonk Highway** | Lower Broadway | Downtown | Strip of live music bars |

## Neighborhood Geography Reference

### CRITICAL CORRECTIONS
- **Cleveland Park** is in **EAST NASHVILLE** near Dickerson Pike (ZIP 37207), NOT near Belmont
- **Belmont** is in **SOUTH NASHVILLE/MIDTOWN** near Belmont University (ZIP 37204)
- These are 6-7 miles apart - completely different parts of town

## Local Slang & Terminology

### Food Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Hot Chicken** | Spicy fried chicken with cayenne - Nashville original since 1930s. Prince's invented it. |
| **Meat and Three** | Restaurant serving protein + 3 vegetable sides. Nashville is the meat-and-three mecca. |
| **Spiced Round** | Beef with cinnamon-sugar-spiced pork fat - vintage Christmas specialty |

### Music Industry Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Gherm** | Someone overly eager to network in music industry - handing out CDs, bragging about gigs |
| **Writers in the Round** | Performance format where songwriters take turns playing their songs |
| **Mother Church** | The Ryman Auditorium |

### Sports Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Preds** | Nashville Predators (NHL) |
| **Smashville** | Predators fan culture / Bridgestone Arena |
| **Titans** | NFL team |
| **Vols** | University of Tennessee athletics |
| **Commodores** | Vanderbilt University athletics |

### Tourism/Local Tension Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Woo Girls** | Bachelorette parties screaming "WOOO!" on party buses/pedal taverns. Locals are... not fans. |
| **Pedal Tavern** | Multi-person pedal-powered party vehicle. Locals stuck behind them in traffic. |
| **Transportainment** | Industry term for party buses, pedal taverns, tractors, barges |
| **Bach Party** / **Bach Industry** | Bachelorette parties and related tourism businesses |
| **Transplant** | Someone who moved to Nashville (vs native) |
| **Unicorn** | Someone actually BORN in Nashville - extremely rare |

### General Nashville/Southern Expressions
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Y'all** | You all (plural you) |
| **Fixin' to** | About to do something |
| **Bless your heart** | Can be sympathy OR subtle insult depending on context |
| **Holler and Swaller** | Toast meaning yell and take a shot |
| **Honky-tonkin'** | Bar-hopping on Broadway |
| **Pike** | Major road (historically toll roads) - Dickerson Pike, Gallatin Pike, etc. |
